SOUR (2021)

Released in May 2021, SOUR is Olivia Rodrigo's monumental debut studio album. The record serves as a vulnerable, genre-blending exploration of teenage heartbreak, jealousy, and insecurity. Seamlessly shifting between explosive alt-pop punk anthems and minimalist piano ballads, it captures the raw turbulence of adolescence.

The album achieved massive critical and commercial success, fueled by record-breaking global hit singles like "drivers license", "deja vu", and "good 4 u". SOUR went on to win three Grammy Awards, solidifying Rodrigo as a defining pop voice of her generation.
