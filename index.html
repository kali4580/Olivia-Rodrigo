The record primarily chronicles the aftermath of a failed romance, capturing a messy spectrum of post-breakup emotions ranging from the deep sorrow and betrayal found in "Drivers License" and "Traitor" to the explosive rage of "Good 4 U." Beyond the core narrative of a lost relationship, the album heavily addresses societal pressures and modern anxieties, with tracks like "Jealousy, Jealousy" and "Brutal" diving into the destructive nature of social media comparison and the intense insecurity of youth. By channeling these bitter, painful experiences into her music, Rodrigo created a relatable coming-of-age soundtrack that transforms personal grief into a powerful collective expression of teenage angst.
