GUTS

Spill Your Guts // 2023

The Thematic Depth of GUTS

Released in September 2023, GUTS represents Olivia Rodrigo's sharp transition into an aggressive sophomore rock sound. The album moves away from acoustic bedroom processing, favoring heavy garage-rock guitars, punching drums, and a deeply sarcastic punk attitude. Thematically, it acts as a unfiltered diary tracking the messy mistakes and fierce social anxieties of entering her twenties.

The explosive record was spearheaded by the sweeping rock ballad "vampire", flanked by cheeky anthems like "bad idea right?" and the high-octane "get him back!". Its amethyst-goth style and lightning-fast tempos proved that her writing style had evolved with brilliant maturity and bite.
