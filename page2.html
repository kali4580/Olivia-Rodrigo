GUTS (2023)

Released in September 2023, GUTS is Olivia Rodrigo's highly anticipated sophomore album. This record expands upon her signature pop-punk sound with sharper wit, heavier guitar riffs, and more sophisticated songwriting. It chronicles the awkward, chaotic transition into her early twenties, navigating sudden fame and messy relationships.

The album was preceded by the chart-topping lead single "vampire", followed by the sarcastic rock anthem "bad idea right?", and the high-energy "get him back!". Highly praised by critics, GUTS proved that her musical instincts were sharper and bolder than ever.
